# Analysis-of-Digital-Banking-Infrastructure-and-Card-Transaction-Behaviour-in-India
## About the Project

This project is an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of banking and digital payment data in India.\

The main purpose of this project is to understand digital banking infrastructure, credit and debit card usage, transaction channels, and changes in payment behaviour from 2022 onwards.

## Objectives
Compare banking infrastructure across bank categories.\
Study credit card and debit card penetration.\
Analyze credit card transaction channels.\
Analyze debit card transaction channels.\
Compare transaction volume and transaction value.\
Study changes in digital payment infrastructure over time.\
Compare banks and bank categories.\
Identify important patterns in card-based transactions and customer payment behaviour.\

## Key Findings\
Private Sector Banks show strong digital-payment infrastructure.\
Debit cards have higher penetration than credit cards.\
Credit card transactions are mainly driven by PoS and Online channels.\
Debit card transaction volume is largely dominated by ATM cash withdrawals.\
Transaction volume and transaction value show different patterns across channels.\
Digital-payment infrastructure, particularly UPI QR and PoS, shows significant changes over the study period.\
Credit and debit card transaction behaviour shows different trends over time.\

## Conclusion\
This project helped me understand the changing nature of banking infrastructure and card-based payment behaviour in India. The analysis shows the growing importance of digital payment infrastructure while also highlighting the continued significance of traditional card and ATM transactions.
